# Privacy Policy — Bisram News

Bisram News is a private tool used only by the owner of the Bisram TV channel (Zahid Ullah Alokozay) to publish news videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

- The app uses Google/YouTube access only to upload videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to the owner's own YouTube channel.
- No data from any other user is collected, stored, sold or shared.
- Access tokens are stored encrypted and are used for no other purpose.
- Access can be removed at any time from https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
